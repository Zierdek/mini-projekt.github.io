The Volvo C30 was offered with a wide range of petrol and diesel engines, from the 100 hp 1.6 up to the 230 hp T5. Every version was front-wheel drive. Newer diesel engines arrived for the 2010–2013 model years.

Petrol engines

Engine Power Notes
1.6 100 hp Base petrol engine
1.8 125 hp Also offered as a flex-fuel version running on E85
2.0 145 hp Also sold as the 2.0F flexifuel version
2.4i 170 hp Five-cylinder, 230 Nm of torque, optional five-speed automatic
T5 (2.5) 220 hp (230 hp from May 2007) Turbocharged five-cylinder, 0–100 km/h in 6.7 s with the manual gearbox

Diesel engines

Engine Power Notes
1.6D / DRIVe 109 hp Economical: about 4.5 l/100 km in the DRIVe version, with start/stop added in spring 2009
2.0D 136 hp 320 Nm of torque, about 5.7 l/100 km
D5 (2.4D) 180 hp Five-cylinder, 400 Nm of torque
D2 (1.6) 115 hp Newer diesel for the 2010–2013 model years
D3 (2.0) 150 hp Newer diesel for the 2010–2013 model years
D4 (2.0) 177 hp Strongest diesel of the later range

Electric version

The C30 Electric was built in limited numbers from 2011 and leased mainly to companies, authorities and government bodies. The cars were assembled in Ghent like the regular C30, then sent to Gothenburg to receive the electric motor and battery.

Version Power Notes
C30 Electric 82 kW (111 hp), 220 Nm 24 kWh battery (22.7 kWh usable), range up to 150 km, top speed 130 km/h, 0–100 km/h in 10.5 s, about 7–8 hours to charge from a household socket
C30 Electric (later version) 89 kW (120 hp) Range up to 163 km (NEDC)

Power figures are manufacturer "hp" values and vary slightly between sources. Sources: Volvo Cars press materials, cars-data.com, autotijd.be.
